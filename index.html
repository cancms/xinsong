X-In Lab

X-scale Inspiration · Integration · Intervention

We are a dynamic and friendly group at Department of Biomedical Engineering, City University of Hong Kong. We develop bio-inspired materials, intelligent robotics, and extracorporeal platforms for precision intervention at the physiological interface.

Research Topics

Materials and interfaces for extracorporeal and intravascular therapies
Cross-scale systems for controlled and targeted medical intervention
Bio-inspired, biohybrid, and bio-derived robotic systems for healthcare
